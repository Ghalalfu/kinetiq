Kinetiq

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Kinetiq is a sports and fitness app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It checks your running, tennis and padel technique from video, tracks your daily activity, scores matches, and lets you share results with friends. This policy explains what information Kinetiq uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short: your videos never leave your iPhone. Your Health data stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share a result or join a challenge. Kinetiq has no ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Kinetiq is made by Ghala Al Fuhaid in Al Khobar, Saudi Arabia (“we”, “us”). If you have questions about this policy or your data, email ghaya.support@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your device

Most of Kinetiq works entirely on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We do not receive this information.

  • Videos for form checks. When you record or choose a video, Kinetiq analyzes it on your iPhone with Apple’s body-pose detection. The copy Kinetiq uses is deleted as soon as the analysis finishes. Videos are never uploaded. Kinetiq only accesses the videos and photos you pick, not your whole library.
  • Form check results. Reports and form scores are kept on your iPhone.
  • Camera in match mode. When you use the virtual court, the camera image is processed live on your iPhone to follow your position. It is not recorded or stored. Only your movement statistics are kept, on your iPhone.
  • Apple Health data. If you allow it, Kinetiq reads steps, distance, active energy, heart rate, sleep and workouts from Apple Health to show your daily activity. Kinetiq never writes to Apple Health and never uses Health data for advertising.
  • Your body profile and meals. The age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal you enter for the calorie calculator, and the meals you log, are kept on your iPhone. The food database is built into the app, so searching it sends nothing anywhere.
  • Matches. Match scores and history are kept on your iPhone, and shared between your iPhone and Apple Watch when you use the watch scorer.
  • Settings. Preferences such as appearance and units are kept on your iPhone.

Information you choose to share in Community

Community is optional. If you sign in, we store the following on servers run for us by Supabase, our hosting provider:

  • Account. Your email address, used to send sign-in links and to identify your account.
  • Profile. Your name, username, bio, favorite sports, whether your profile is private, and your profile photo if you add one. The photo is cropped and resized on your iPhone before upload.
  • Shared activities. Workouts, matches and form checks that you choose to share: the type of activity, date, duration, distance, and short details such as average heart rate or the match score. Only the numbers are shared, never videos or routes.
  • Challenges and awards. When you join a challenge, Kinetiq calculates your total on your iPhone from your Health workouts, matches or form scores, and sends only that total (for example “23.4 km” or “form score 82”). Awards you earn are stored with your profile.
  • Social connections. Who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, the clubs you create or join, and people you block.
  • Reports. If you report a person or club, we store your report so we can review it.

Who can see it. Your name, username, photo and bio can be found by other signed-in users, except people you have blocked. Shared activities, challenge progress and awards are visible to signed-in users if your profile is public. If your profile is private, they are visible only to followers you approve and to members of clubs you belong to. Blocked users cannot see your profile or activity.

Purchases

Kinetiq Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription so Pro features can be unlocked.

How we use information

  • To provide the app’s features, such as form analysis, activity tracking, Community, challenges and clubs.
  • To keep Community safe: handling reports, enforcing our Terms, and preventing abuse.
  • To respond when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host Community data, including the database, sign-in emails and profile photos. Supabase processes this data only to provide its service to us. Apple provides App Store purchases, Apple Health and Apple Watch features under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Information on your device stays until you delete it or delete the app. Community information is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photo, shared activities, follows, challenge entries, awards and clubs you own are deleted from our servers. Reports you made may be kept for a short time if they are needed to deal with abuse.

Your choices and rights

  • Health access. You can turn Kinetiq’s Health access on or off at any time in the Health app or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.
  • Sharing. Nothing is shared unless you share it. You can remove any shared activity from your profile, leave challenges and clubs, make your profile private, and block people.
  • Edit or delete your account. Edit your profile in Community › Settings. Delete your account in Community › Settings › Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately.
  • Access and other rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Email ghaya.support@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Community data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by database rules so that each person can only change their own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Kinetiq is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 should not use Community. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

International users

Kinetiq is available worldwide. Community data may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, where Supabase operates its servers.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app. Continuing to use Kinetiq after a change means you accept the updated policy.
